# Forensic directions: investigator + verifier (raw)

## options — אופציות ומסחר מוקדם

### Summary
I looked for signs that anyone traded on advance knowledge before the three events: the NYT report on Oct 29, the sector crash on Nov 5, and Huang's admission and the board probe on Nov 8/9. I found no evidence that anyone did. The best timing test is the weekend: GlobeNewswire time-stamped the board statement at 15:00 ET on Sunday Nov 8, and the first bearish bets on NVDA perpetual futures (24/7 crypto-exchange contracts) were reported at about 3:00 p.m. ET. So the selling started at publication, not before it. The best-known weekend short, about $5.4M on Hyperliquid, came about 2.5 hours after the memo. NVDA options flow in the days before each event leaned toward calls (bullish) or was below average. The record put volume on Nov 5 (11.23M contracts on Cboe) came during the crash, not before it, and NVDA fell less than its peers that day (-9.26% vs SOX -12.25%, AMD -17.26%). On Nov 9 the biggest trade was a bullish call spread of about $57M, set against only about $10M of premarket put premium. The real weaknesses are structural: many people had the information early (an intelligence memo shared with top officials, a reported year-old briefing of Trump, and Nvidia knowing the NYT story was coming), and a large share of weekend price discovery ran on offshore perpetual-futures venues that are hard to surveil. I could not get minute-level, strike-level or dark-pool data, so I cannot rule out small, well-hidden informed trades.
